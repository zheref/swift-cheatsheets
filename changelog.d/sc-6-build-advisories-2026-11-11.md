### Security

- **Five of the seven HIGH build-time advisories under Gatsby 5.16.1 are fixed, and the other two are recorded as accepted (zheref/swift-cheatsheets#6).** New `package.json` overrides move `serialize-javascript` to 7.1.4, `sharp` (via `gatsby-sharp`, which `gatsby-plugin-utils` depends on) to 0.35.5 and `webpack-dev-middleware` to 7.4.6, each checked against the Gatsby code that calls it; the built pages and the offline `index.html` are byte-identical. `@graphql-tools/utils` (typegen only, which is off) and `decode-uri-component` (the develop-only 404 page) have no fix their callers can load, so [`docs/DEPENDENCY-ADVISORIES.md`](docs/DEPENDENCY-ADVISORIES.md) records them with their reachability, an owner and what would reopen them. The dependency audit goes from 7 HIGH findings to 2.
