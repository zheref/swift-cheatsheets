### Fixed

- **The accessibility and token gaps the page inherited from the legacy one are closed (zheref/swift-cheatsheets#5).** Every control's boundary — the search field, the bar buttons, the language toggle, the copy button, the notice's chips and Dismiss — now uses a new `edge` colour at 3:1 or more against the page and its fill in both themes, where it was 1.26:1 (dark) and 1.18:1 (light). The type scale is eight rem steps, so the page's text follows the reader's preferred size; desktop keeps the dense scale, and narrow screens get nothing below 12px and 16px body text, as the maintainer ruled (diagrams, being drawn images, keep their own label sizes — tracked in zheref/swift-cheatsheets#13). On narrow screens the search field takes its own row, and every bar control is touch-sized on any coarse pointer. Spacing is one rem scale shared by the primitives and the page. The search field keeps a visible "Search" label and icon while the reader types. The menu, theme, caret and check marks are vector icons rather than font glyphs, and the theme button shows the theme it switches to — a sun on the dark page, a moon on the light one. The page's components write no colour, font-size or spacing literal: they read type roles and tokens defined once in the theme.
