### Added

- **A language selector in the top bar, and catalogs scoped by language (zheref/swift-cheatsheets#2).** `content/registry.yaml` lists the four configured languages — Swift, TypeScript, Kotlin, C# — and every page's selector offers them in that order: a keyboard- and screen-reader-operable disclosure of plain links that also works with scripts off. Switching keeps the reader on the same concept wherever the other language has it; where it does not, the page they land on names the missing topic and offers related ones. Language and topic live in the address, so deep links, reload and back/forward restore them. Search, its count and empty state, and snippet highlighting cover the active language only. Only Swift is published today: TypeScript, Kotlin and C# are listed as not yet published until their catalogs land (#3). Four fixture catalogs prove the switching for all four languages and say on the page that they are test data.
