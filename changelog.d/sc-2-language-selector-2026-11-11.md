### Added

- **A language selector in the top bar, and catalogs scoped by language (zheref/swift-cheatsheets#2).** `content/registry.yaml` lists the four configured languages — Swift, TypeScript, Kotlin, C# — and every page's selector offers them in that order: a keyboard- and screen-reader-operable disclosure of plain links that also works with scripts off. Switching keeps the reader on the same concept wherever the other language has it; where it does not, the page they land on names the missing topic and offers related ones. Language and topic live in the address, so deep links, reload and back/forward restore them. Search, its count and empty state, and snippet highlighting cover the active language only. A language whose catalog is not built is listed as not yet published, never as an empty page. Four fixture catalogs prove the switching for all four languages and say on the page that they are test data.
